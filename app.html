Your quiver

Kite sizing uses the widely quoted weight x 2.2 / wind rule of thumb - it lands mid-chart for many brands, but brand charts, kite type, board, skill and gusts all shift the answer. When in doubt take the smaller kite, and never trust an app over the beach briefing.
